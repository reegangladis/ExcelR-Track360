## 1. Governance & Purpose

In adherence to Mandatory Development Rule #13 (*"Record unresolved business questions instead of inventing answers"*), this document catalogues critical business decisions, operational ambiguities, and policy forks identified during Phase 1 analysis. 

Each question outlines the operational impact, viable technical and procedural options with tradeoffs, a recommended architectural direction, and the designated organizational authority required for formal sign-off.

## 2. Unresolved Business Questions Registry

| Question ID | Business Domain | Ambiguity / Decision Needed | Architectural Impact | Proposed Options & Tradeoffs | Recommended Direction | Required Approver | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OBQ-01** | Certificate Management | **Certificate Issuance Cadence:** Should certificates be issued incrementally after each completed project milestone, or exclusively as a single cumulative exit certificate? | High: Dictates database schema for certificate models, serial numbering, PDF templates, and student portal credential views. | **Option A (Incremental Milestone):** Issue a 3-month cert after P1, 6-month cert after P2, 9-month cert after P3.<br>*Pros:* High student motivation.<br>*Cons:* Multiple active certs per student; administrative overhead.<br>**Option B (Final Exit Only):** Issue exactly one certificate when candidate concludes program, reflecting total completed duration.<br>*Pros:* Higher certificate prestige, cleaner registry.<br>*Cons:* Delayed gratification for learners. | **Option B with early exit exception:** Default to single comprehensive exit credential, but allow early issuance if a candidate formally departs after Project 1 or 2. | Academic Director / Head of Operations | **OPEN** |
| **OBQ-02** | Group Readiness & Progression | **Inactive / Lagging Candidate Protocol:** What is the precise business threshold and procedure for unblocking a collaborative group when 1 member fails to submit/request? | High: Governs state machine timeouts, automated email reminders, and group reconstitution workflows. | **Option A (Automated Grace Period & Drop):** If a student fails to request progression within 7 days of deliverable approval, system automatically drops them from the active group to `UNASSIGNED` and advances remaining ready members.<br>*Pros:* Zero coordinator bottleneck.<br>*Cons:* Potential student friction if legitimate delay.<br>**Option B (Manual Coordinator Intervention):** Group blocks indefinitely until Project Coordinator manually splits group or executes an administrative override.<br>*Pros:* Human discretion.<br>*Cons:* Group progress stalled if coordinator is slow. | **Option C (Hybrid):** Automated daily reminder notifications for 5 days. On Day 6, coordinator receives an urgent action alert. Coordinator executes 1-click group split or override. | Project Operations Lead | **OPEN** |
| **OBQ-03** | Internship Milestone Management | **Internship Start Date & Calendar Coupling:** How is the official internship start date determined for a candidate? | Medium: Affects internship entity schema, automated calculation vs manual entry, and date display on public verification. | **Option A (Automated from Project 1 Kickoff):** Internship start date automatically matches the kickoff date of Project 1.<br>*Pros:* Fully automated.<br>*Cons:* Inflexible if corporate internship began earlier or later.<br>**Option B (Manual Entry by Coordinator):** Coordinator manually inputs calendar start and end dates based on corporate partner agreements.<br>*Pros:* 100% accurate to real-world corporate contracts.<br>*Cons:* Requires coordinator manual data entry. | **Option B with smart default:** System defaults start date to Project 1 allocation date, but requires Internship Coordinator to review and confirm/adjust before completion. | Internship Coordinator Lead | **OPEN** |
| **OBQ-04** | Assignment Management | **Resubmission Attempt Limits & Scoring:** Are resubmissions after `CORRECTIONS_REQUESTED` unlimited until passing, and how is the final recorded score computed? | Medium: Impacts assignment attempt counters, student portal submission locking, and grade aggregation logic. | **Option A (Unlimited Attempts, Latest Score Counts):** Students resubmit until passing score is achieved. Latest score replaces prior scores.<br>*Pros:* Mastery-based learning; encourages improvement.<br>*Cons:* Evaluator burnout if students submit low-effort revisions.<br>**Option B (Capped at 3 Attempts, Latest Score):** Maximum 3 attempts permitted. If attempt 3 fails, assignment is locked and flagged for coordinator review.<br>*Pros:* Prevents evaluator fatigue; enforces discipline.<br>*Cons:* Requires administrative unlock for edge cases. | **Option B (Capped at 3 Attempts):** Standard policy caps attempts at 3, with coordinator unlock capability for extenuating circumstances. | Academic Operations Manager | **OPEN** |
| **OBQ-05** | Project Management | **Group Project Evaluation Model:** Does a project receive a single uniform evaluation for all group members, or can individual member scores diverge? | Medium: Influences group deliverable review schema, rubric submission interface, and individual student progress metrics. | **Option A (Uniform Group Grade):** All active group members receive the identical score and pass/fail outcome for the deliverable.<br>*Pros:* Simple, fosters genuine team accountability.<br>*Cons:* Penalizes high contributors if peers slacked off.<br>**Option B (Individual Assessment Within Group):** Reviewer evaluates group deliverable as a whole, but can assign individual contribution modifiers or individual passing marks.<br>*Pros:* Fair to high achievers.<br>*Cons:* Reviewer friction evaluating individual commits/contributions. | **Option A as default with individual exception toggle:** Default to uniform group outcome, but permit reviewer to adjust individual status if non-participation is documented. | Head of Training / Project Lead | **OPEN** |
| **OBQ-06** | Project Management | **Criteria for 4th Project Extension:** Under what exact organizational criteria can a candidate be permitted a 4th project beyond the standard maximum of 3? | Low: Impacts backend business validation rules and authorization role checks. | **Option A (Strict Disallowance):** System hard-blocks any 4th project under all circumstances.<br>**Option B (Executive Administrative Override):** System allows 4th project only if approved by `Super Administrator` with mandatory academic justification note (e.g. corporate sponsorship, dual-track specialization). | **Option B:** Allow Super Admin policy override with mandatory audit logging. | Managing Director | **OPEN** |
| **OBQ-07** | Certificate Management | **Certificate Revocation Policy & Public UI:** When an issued certificate is revoked, what exact information should be rendered on the public verification page? | Low: Impacts public verification template and legal compliance. | **Option A (Generic Revocation Notice):** Displays "Certificate Revoked" without specific rationale.<br>*Pros:* Protects candidate privacy.<br>**Option B (Detailed Reason Display):** Displays "Certificate Revoked: Plagiarism Detected / Academic Misconduct" or "Revoked: Re-issued due to name correction".<br>*Pros:* Absolute transparency for employers.<br>*Cons:* Potential defamation/privacy concerns if disputed. | **Option A with internal notes:** Public page displays "Status: Revoked - Please contact ExcelR Verification Services for details." Detailed reason is visible internally only. | Legal & Compliance Officer | **OPEN** |
| **OBQ-08** | Platform Operations | **Student Portal Access Retention Post-Completion (Alumni Policy):** How long does a candidate retain access to the active learning portal after completing the program? | Low: Determines user deactivation cron jobs, session policies, and long-term storage access. | **Option A (Lifetime Access):** Candidate account remains in `ALUMNI` state indefinitely with read-only access to their portfolio, submissions, and credentials.<br>**Option B (1-Year Retention Post-Completion):** Access expires 12 months after certificate issuance, after which only public verification remains active. | **Option A:** Maintain indefinite read-only `ALUMNI` portal access for credential and portfolio access, which enhances alumni loyalty and employment verification. | Head of Operations | **OPEN** |

## 3. Decision Action Plan & Next Steps

1. **Stakeholder Alignment Meeting:** Schedule a formal review with Academic Operations and Project Leads during Phase 2 to walk through OBQ-01 through OBQ-05.
2. **Configuration-Driven Flexibility:** For all open questions above, the application architecture shall implement **configurable policy variables** in the database rather than hardcoding business assumptions into application code.
3. **Audit Trail Logging:** As formal decisions are resolved, they will be updated in this document with approver name, decision date, and migration instructions.
